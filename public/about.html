About Us

About Trishul Divine

Trishul Divine was born from a reverence for ancient symbolism and a devotion to modern craftsmanship — where every piece carries intention, and every detail is considered.

Our Story

What began as a quiet fascination with the trident's timeless form has grown into a pursuit of meaning through design. We set out to create pieces that honor tradition without being bound by it — objects that feel both ancestral and entirely contemporary.

Each creation is shaped with patience, guided by craftsmanship passed down through generations and refined for a modern eye. We believe true luxury lies not in excess, but in restraint — in the space left for reflection.

Our Philosophy

We approach every design as a quiet ritual — a balance of form, material, and intention. Nothing is added without purpose; nothing is removed without care.

At Trishul Divine, spirituality is not a statement — it is a presence. We create for those who seek meaning in simplicity, and elegance in the eternal.
