How to Get Help

  • Product questions and recommendations – message us on WhatsApp for the quickest reply.
  • Order status and tracking – share your order number and the phone number used at checkout.
  • Cancellations, returns and refunds – follow the steps in our Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy.
  • Payment issues – if money was deducted but you did not get an order confirmation, send us the payment reference and we will check it for you.

Raising a Complaint

If you are not satisfied with a product or our service, email trishuldivine26@gmail.com with the subject “Complaint”, your order number and a short description of the issue, with photos where helpful.

  • We acknowledge every complaint within 48 hours.
  • We aim to resolve complaints within 7 business days, and will keep you updated if it takes longer.
  • If you are not happy with the outcome, reply to the same email asking for a review, and a senior team member will look into it.

Response Times

  • Email: within 1 business day.
